# Parked Car Review

## Concept
An honest, unpolished food review filmed from a parked car with a phone on the dashboard. This module reviews the finished dish as takeout, so the cooking steps are skipped or told in a sentence. Authenticity is the entire point.

## Story arc
1. Short context and a candid first impression of the container. Face and hands are clean.
2. Visual unboxing: the reviewer holds the food a few inches from the phone lens so it fills most of the frame, turning it slowly to show it, then pulls back. A little sauce is on the fingertips.
3. Live, uncut first bite, then the bitten food is held close to the lens to show the inside (never torn or pulled apart). Sauce is on the lips and a smear on the chin.
4. Talking with the mouth full, mid-chew, giving an honest reaction. Sauce on the lips, chin and one cheek, greasy shiny fingers, and a dab on the side of the nose.
5. Wiping the face with a napkin, giving the final take and the spoken score out of 10. The wipe only smears it around: sauce is still visible on the cheek and nose.

## Running gag
The reviewer gets steadily messier through the review, whatever the score. The food's own sauce, grease or crumbs build up on the lips, chin, cheeks, an occasional spot on the nose, and on the fingers and hands, a little more in every segment. Use the actual colors and textures of the dish (red broth for birria, marinara for pasta, glaze for wings). The reviewer never notices or mentions the mess and keeps a completely straight, serious face. The napkin never fully fixes it. Keep it silly and clean enough for any audience: sauce, grease and crumbs only.

## Character on screen
Face fully visible, chest-up against the headrest, speaking naturally to camera. Leans forward to hold the container inches from the lens, then pulls back to eat.

## Visual prompt tokens
- **Framing:** static dashboard-mounted phone, vertical, chest-up; manual lean-in for the food
- **Lens and depth of field:** smartphone wide angle, about 24mm, deep depth of field with slight wide-angle distortion
- **Lighting:** unmodified natural daylight through the windshield and windows, harsh falloff, whatever the sun does
- **Set and props:** real car interior, seat headrest, takeout container
- **Color:** no color grade, ordinary phone auto exposure and white balance

## Gear notes
Smartphone front camera, small wireless lavalier clipped on or held close.

## Audio direction
Dry, close-up voice in a sound-dampened car cabin. No music, no sound effects.

## Edit rules
- Long unscripted takes of 6-25s joined by hard jump cuts
- Keep real-time chewing and reflective pauses
- No B-roll, no secondary camera

## Avoid
Studio lighting, cinematic bokeh, color grading, B-roll cutaways, background music, a clean face at the end, the reviewer commenting on their own mess.
